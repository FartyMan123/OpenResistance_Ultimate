## Regional support

As agreed with Jasper, each military site joins its nearest town/city's region. Distance is horizontal; stable IDs break ties. `m_sSupportRegionId` on a location overrides this assignment; several settlements can share a region ID. For generated Chicken layers, edit the CSV `RegionId` column before regeneration (blank uses the automatic assignment). It is scenario data, not a saved campaign ownership map.

Every 60 seconds after campaign setup, count the region's flag-capturable installations by controlling role. Their equal shares define the target support distribution. Move each settlement toward that target by at most two percentage points for any role per cycle. Interpolating all three values together keeps their sum at 100 without granting support to an absent role. A region with no installations has no military influence and retains support. The original initial support, >50% ownership rule, fixed control and flip cooldown remain authoritative. From 0% Resistance support, complete regional military control takes 26 default cycles to exceed 50%; a 50/50 split does not itself produce a majority.

Regional timing/step are attributes on `AROR_RegionalSupportComponent`. This supplies ongoing military influence rather than a repeated capture-event bonus, so recaptures reverse pressure and reload does not award historical bonuses. Existing support serialization and map UI are reused. Other support influences remain possible through the existing API.

## Defence recipes

| Location | Default recipe |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary outpost | 8 infantry, 1 crewed MG; no vehicles even under a stronger infantry override |
| Military outpost/base | 24 infantry, 3 crewed MGs, 1 parked unmanned LAV/BTR, 1 random crewed vehicle |
| Resources / factory / port | Roll level 1–3 once: 8/16/24 infantry, 1/2/3 MGs, 0/1/1 crewed vehicles; level 3 also has parked armour |
| Airbase | 32 infantry, 4 MGs, parked armour, 3 random crewed vehicles |

All recipes use four-person infantry teams. Crew is additional and comes from the actual non-cargo compartments; seating is requested directly and confirmed before defend orders. Occupier random hulls are LAV-25/M2 Humvee; Invaders use BTR-70/BRDM-2 with equivalent parked BTR and NSV roles. Each role's assets and native faction are configured independently in `AROR_GarrisonFaction`, checked against the shared role mapping. This does not enable Invader QRF admission; garrisons follow the actual site controller and their own valid binding.

These are provisional strengths. Infantry currently uses the configured rifleman character rather than specialist fireteam compositions. [Six military composition presets](MILITARY_DEFENCE_PRESETS.md) now supply authored sandbag perimeters and editable defence positions; they are library resources pending native site acceptance, not automatically stamped into Chicken. Legacy sites use matching compact positions around their flags. Whole-footprint relief/water, local obstruction/navmesh and MG firing-arc checks replace the old wide random scatter. Building-interior occupation and runtime fortification construction are not implemented. A clear pad is not proof of practical driving access; audit each site in Play.

## Activation, losses and capture

The manager checks current living player-controlled character positions every 250 ms against the site centre. Activation defaults to 1,000 m and works when a player teleports directly inside a site; it does not depend on crossing the radius. Defender pads need only 6 m of player clearance rather than the former 150 m exclusion. Standing at the central flag therefore allows its perimeter defenders to start constructing. One hull or one team request is admitted per quarter-second across sites; the native shared queue constructs personnel asynchronously. Terrain, navigation, obstruction checks, preserved casualties and capacity limits still apply, so a complete roster is not guaranteed to appear within one tick. A shared default cap of 96 conservative personnel reservations covers active or constructing garrisons, including estimated crew before hull creation. QRF capacity remains separate; consider both when tuning server load. Blocked pads must not prevent foot teams or other sites from being considered.

The 12:14 native Levie Military Outpost run confirms proximity admission but a 2.78125m whole-footprint terrain rejection. Chicken now authors a 3m allowance at that site only through `DefenceMaxRelief`, retaining its full military layout. Individual pad and navigation gates remain active. Reload the scene and retest activation; the prior log establishes the rejected height range, not successful deployment under the new allowance.

The subsequent 12:19 run confirms actual Levie infantry, MG and vehicle deployment. It exposed a separate capture blocker: unspawned assets retried blocked pads for minutes. Once real personnel have deployed and submitted native queues have resolved, unused recipe positions no longer prevent a capture; actual living hostiles in the location radius still do. Failed pad placement has a configurable retry window (30 seconds by default), after which that unqueued asset/team and dependent crew are omitted for this activation. This is not a death or a serialized loss. Those positions cannot subsequently materialise in that activation; retirement resets their placement attempts. Native queues and live actors cannot be discarded by this timeout.

Chicken's QRF sequence is **clear the outpost, capture the flag, then request the QRF**. `m_bCombatQRFs` defaults off and is explicitly off in Chicken: hostile damage reports do not create requests or feed threat/contact side effects through the damage adapter. Enabled canonical capture events still queue the previous hostile owner's response, subject to funding, cooldowns and capacity. Settlement support flips also retain their separately enabled capture-response path. Extenders can explicitly re-enable combat responses. Existing operations from a previous Play session are not retroactively cancelled.

Retirement defaults to 2,500 m and 120 continuous seconds. Every player, including spectators with a controlled entity or dead characters, must be far enough from both site and tracked actors before deletion. Returning resets the grace timer. Native player control and foreign vehicle occupants prevent cleanup ownership from being taken away. Explicitly transferred assets become unavailable roster slots; moving a remaining hull outside the site also prevents its later recreation. Group queues are cancelled during retirement, and living administrative retirement is not counted as a death.

Casualties, destroyed/missing previously physical assets and coarse health depletion are retained. Unspawned virtual slots are not losses. They do not refill when the player leaves and returns. The per-location serializer writes the actual chosen resources, group/seat relations, depleted flags and placement results, not a random seed. Loaded rosters do not resume live orders; they reactivate near players. Individual inventory, ammo, fuel and exact hit-zone health are not preserved. Captured assets left in the world need separate entity persistence for durable loot restoration; the roster only guarantees that it will not generate a replacement.

Ownership changes clear QRF defence assignments. Existing physical survivors retire under the same distance rules; an opposing new owner gets a new roster only after safe retirement. Resistance does not receive generated friendly defenders. There is no periodic replacement purchase, bank debit or economy/stock provider in this change. Existing QRF finance is unaffected. The military capture interaction treats unmaterialised surviving defenders as pending, so a teleport to a flag cannot exploit the native spawn queue. Disabled/missing faction bindings do not install that pending-defence gate.

## Current perimeter bindings

Chicken now binds all 23 military sites to perimeter presets: 20 measured placements and three compact drafts. Strategic roots remain fixed where required; translated children follow their flag, and runtime/editor checks keep anchored perimeters inside the canonical capture circle. Four compact variants retain recipe roles with separated infantry/MG/vehicle pads. Standard cardinal teams are moved inward to avoid the former MG clearance conflict.
